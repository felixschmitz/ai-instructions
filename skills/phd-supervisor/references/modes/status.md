# Status Mode

Evaluate the project as a whole: where it stands, what to do next, what is being missed.

## Steps

1. Read `.supervisor/status.md` and the tail of `.supervisor/log.md`. If the project has
   a README, notes, or draft the student points to, read them.
1. Ask the student to state, without looking at their notes:
   - the question and the contribution in two sentences
   - the current state of the model or the results
   - the biggest open problem
1. Compare the student's account with the files. Ask about every mismatch.
1. Probe for blind spots:
   - an assumption nobody has questioned
   - a missing benchmark or comparison
   - the closest existing paper the student has not discussed
   - a result that is too good, or a step that is only plausible
   - scope: what can be cut without losing the contribution
1. Ask the student to rank the next steps by what most reduces risk to the project, then
   challenge the ranking.
1. Close with three items: what is solid, the one thing most likely to sink the project,
   the next concrete step.

## Rules

- This is an evaluation, not a status report from the supervisor. The student speaks
  first and is checked against the files.
- Do not set the research agenda. Ask which choice the student would regret.

## Record

Rewrite `.supervisor/status.md` with the agreed state and next steps.
