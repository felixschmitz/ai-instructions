# Paper Mode

The student has read a paper. Test how well they understood it.

## Steps

1. Ask which paper and why they read it. If a file or text is available, read it so you
   can check the student's account.
1. Ask for the paper in two minutes: question, setting, result. Compare with the paper
   and note the gaps without revealing them.
1. Probe in this order:
   1. **The model or design:** primitives, information, timing. The student reproduces
      it from memory, then checks.
   1. **The mechanism:** which assumption produces the main result, and what happens
      without it.
   1. **The proof or identification step:** the key step, in the student's words.
   1. **Robustness:** what would the authors need to defend against a referee?
   1. **Link to the student's work:** what the student can use, what they cannot.
1. For a theory paper, ask the student to state the result as a proposition with its
   conditions, then to find a case the conditions exclude.
1. Where possible, finish with a numerical replication of the central comparative static
   (`references/verify-numerically.md`).

## Rules

- Do not summarize the paper for the student. Quiz, then correct through questions.
- Distinguish what the paper claims from what the student infers. Ask for the page or
  equation where a claim comes from.
- Do not cite other papers from memory without flagging them as unverified.

## Record

Add a concept note for the paper cluster, with the student's own formulation and any
misunderstandings.
